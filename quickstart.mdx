## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

Before you begin, ensure you have:
- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- An active email address for account verification
- Optional: A Google or GitHub account for faster sign-up

</Callout>

## Create Your Val Account

Sign up takes under 2 minutes. Choose your preferred method below.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email" icon="mail">
    Visit [https://app.val.com/signup](https://app.val.com/signup).
    
    1. Enter your email and create a password.
    2. Check your inbox for a verification link.
    3. Click the link to activate your account.
    
    You'll land on the dashboard immediately.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google" icon="google">
    Click "Sign up with Google" on [https://app.val.com/signup](https://app.val.com/signup).
    
    Authorize Val to access your basic profile info. No additional permissions required.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="GitHub" icon="github">
    Select "Sign up with GitHub" on [https://app.val.com/signup](https://app.val.com/signup).
    
    Grant repo read access if you plan to import projects later.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  New accounts include 14 days of full access to all features. Upgrade anytime via the billing section.
</Callout>

## Set Up Your First Documentation Space

Follow these steps to create and populate your first space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a New Space" icon="plus">
    From the dashboard, click **New Space**.
    
    Enter a name like "My Project Docs" and select a template (e.g., "API Reference").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your First Page" icon="file-text">
    Inside the space, click **New Page**.
    
    Choose "Blank MDX" or a starter like "Introduction".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and Share" icon="globe">
    Toggle **Publish** in the top-right.
    
    Copy the shareable URL: `https://docs.val.com/my-project-docs/introduction`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Basic Project Organization Tips

Organize effectively from day one to scale effortlessly.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Folder Structure Best Practices" default-open="true">
    Use folders for logical grouping:
    
    - `/introduction` - Landing page
    - `/quickstart` - Setup guides
    - `/api` - Endpoint references
    - `/faq` - Common questions
    
    Drag and drop pages in the sidebar to reorder.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Versioning Workspaces">
    Create separate spaces for major versions (e.g., "v1-docs", "v2-docs").
    
    Use the Versions tab to snapshot changes.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

Here's a simple MDX frontmatter example for your pages:

<CodeGroup tabs="MDX">
  ```mdx
  ---
  title: My Page
  description: Page summary here.
  ---
  
  ## Overview
  
  Content starts here.
  ```
</CodeGroup>

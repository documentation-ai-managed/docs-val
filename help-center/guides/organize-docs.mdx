## Overview
Structure docs for easy navigation in Val.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Folders">
    On your project sidebar, click **New Folder**. Name it, e.g., `API Reference` or `User Guides`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Pages">
    Within a folder, click **Add Page** to create new docs. Drag pages to reorder if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Titles & Tags">
    Give clear titles and add tags for searchability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Hit **Publish All** to update changes for your team or users.
  </Step>
</Steps>

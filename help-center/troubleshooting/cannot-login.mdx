---
title: Unable to Log In to Val
description: Quick solution for login issues in Val.
---

## Symptoms
- Error: "Invalid email or password"
- Stuck on login screen

## Causes
- Mistyped credentials
- Account not verified

## Quick Fix
<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Credentials">
    Double-check your email and password. Passwords are case sensitive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Account">
    Ensure you've verified your email. Check spam for validation emails from noreply@valdocs.com.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset Password">
    Click **Forgot Password** to reset your credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>
